AI Bookmark Organization Rules v4

Goal: group the bookmarks below into folders that make each one easy for a person to find again.

1) OUTPUT FORMAT

Reply with one minified JSON object and nothing else (no markdown, no comments):
{"folders":[{"name":"<folder-slug>","bookmarks":["<url>","<url>"]}]}

- List each bookmark by its URL only, copied exactly as given (no titles, no other fields, no URL changes).
- Every input URL appears exactly once in the whole output: none missing, none repeated, none invented.
- Order does not matter; counts and totals are not needed.

2) HARD LIMITS (outputs that break them are discarded)

- No folder has more than 20 bookmarks: split a big topic into more specific folders.
- At most 2 folders with a single bookmark and at most 3 folders with two bookmarks in the whole output:
  merge small groups into the closest broader topic instead.
- No catch-all folders: never name a folder misc, general, other, uncategorized, various or homepages.
  A bookmark that fits nowhere goes into the closest real topic, even a loose fit.
- Group by meaning, never by language or country: no folders like "german-sites" or "foreign".
  Links on the same topic share a folder whatever their language.

3) CHOOSING FOLDERS

Use the title, description, preview and URL of each bookmark as clues to what it is about and what kind of page it is.
- Be specific where the group allows it: "sourdough-baking" rather than "cooking", "python-libraries" rather than "programming".
- Be consistent: similar bookmarks share the same folder, with one name for one topic.
- Folder count: stick to the number of folders given with the bookmarks below (about one per 8 bookmarks), so most
  folders hold 5 to 20 bookmarks. With few bookmarks, use broad topics ("technology", "business-finance",
  "entertainment", "science", "government-law") instead of one folder per bookmark.
- A site's homepage goes under the site's topic or the entity's name (e.g. "robotics-automation", "npr"), never "homepage".
- Multimedia and code: tutorials in video form can use "-video" (e.g. "woodworking-video"); a GitHub repository is usually a tool or project on its topic; a gist is "code-snippets".
- No clear signals: decide from the URL's domain and path.

4) FOLDER NAMES (SLUGS)

- English, lowercase, words joined by single hyphens, at most 3 words: "<topic>" or "<topic>-<specifier>".
- The specifier is optional and only added when it helps: a sub-topic or the kind of page ("guide", "recipes", "tools", "news", "reviews").
- No redundant words ("links", "sites", "pages", "resources", "articles").
- Titles stay in their original language; only folder names are English.
